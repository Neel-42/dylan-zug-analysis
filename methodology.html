Dataset & Methodology

What matches are included, what was excluded, and how the deuce/ad and pie-chart numbers were derived.

Why hard court only

  • Dylan plays on hard courts for UVA dual matches, ITA events, and NCAA championships (Boar's Head, Tulsa, Athens).
  • The earlier Zug Open page used clay Challenger data — useful for pro development but not representative of his college surface.
  • Clay matches (Zug, Gstaad, ITF clay, etc.) are excluded from this dataset.

Match tiers

  • Tier A — ATP Challenger hard matches with official serve/return stats from Tennis Abstract (aces, 1st serve %, points won, dominance ratio).
  • Tier B — NCAA / ITA / dual matches with final score only. No public point-by-point or serve stats. Pie charts use a games-won percentage as a rough proxy for points won (clearly labeled on the coach page).

Deuce vs ad conclusions

  • Only one hard-court match has full deuce/ad charting: 2024 Charlottesville vs Zachary Svajda (Tennis Abstract charting).
  • Serve: Ad side 81.8% pts won vs Deuce 69.2%.
  • Return: Ad side 44.8% vs Deuce 41.9%.
  • Wide serve tendency on hard: 72.7% deuce-wide vs 50.0% ad-wide (charted match only).
  • Do not apply Zug clay deuce/ad patterns to hard court — on clay his deuce-wide ace pattern was stronger; hard-court charting shows ad-side serve effectiveness.

Points-won percentages (Tier A)

  • Estimated from Tennis Abstract dominance ratio where available: DR ÷ (1 + DR) ≈ share of total points won.
  • Cross-checked against published totals on TennisLive / 365scores when available.

Points-won percentages (Tier B)

  • Estimated as games won ÷ total games played from the final score (e.g. 6-4, 6-4 → 12/20 = 60%).
  • This approximates point share but is not as accurate as official stats — treat as directional only.

Video clips

  • Embedded clips use YouTube start/end parameters — they show one trend (break point, serving stretch, clincher), not full matches.
  • Link-only entries (e.g. ITA Indoors full stream, Clarke highlights reel) — no short public clip found; link goes to full source.
  • No footage — most dual matches and several Challenger rounds have no public video; stats/recap links only.
  • Primary sources: NCAA Championships, Virginia Sports TV, NewYCPhoto (Zheng match), ITA / ChallengerTV YouTube channels.
